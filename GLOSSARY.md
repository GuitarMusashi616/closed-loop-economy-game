# Closed-Loop Economy Game

A web-based, non-spatial game in which the player, as head of government, steers a fully tracked closed-loop economy. The aim is to keep citizens alive and the economy healthy, and to teach how money is created as debt and circulated.

## Language

**Government**:
The player's role, which controls the Treasury, the Central Bank, and the policy of the Commercial Banks.
_Avoid_: Planner, player character, state

**Treasury**:
The part of the Government that collects taxes, spends, and makes Grants and Industry Loans.
_Avoid_: Finance ministry, budget

**Central Bank**:
The part of the Government that sets the Policy Rate and creates money by Monetization.
_Avoid_: Fed, reserve

**Commercial Bank**:
A sector that creates deposits by lending to Industries, under policy set by the Government.
_Avoid_: Bank (unqualified), lender

**Monetization**:
The Central Bank creating new money to fund Government spending or lending.
_Avoid_: Printing, QE

**Policy Rate**:
The interest rate the Central Bank sets, which feeds through to Commercial Bank lending rates.
_Avoid_: Federal rate, base rate

**Grant**:
A one-off cash injection from the Government, to Industries or Households, that is never repeated automatically.
_Avoid_: Stimulus, subsidy, bailout

**Industry Loan**:
Money the Government lends to an Industry, repayable with interest.
_Avoid_: Investment, funding

**Industry**:
A producing firm sector that hires workers, pays wages, makes products, and sells them.
_Avoid_: Company, corporation, business

**Industry Policy**:
The settings the player gives an Industry, which otherwise runs on supply and demand by default.
_Avoid_: Strategy, AI, rules

**Markup / Discount**:
A percentage, defaulting to 0%, that the player sets per Product or per wage and applies after the market price or wage is discovered.
_Avoid_: Price stance, wage stance, subsidy

**Citizen Loan**:
Money lent to a Household Cohort to buy something, at a rate set by what it buys.
_Avoid_: Credit, mortgage (unless collateralised)

**Collateralised Loan**:
A Citizen Loan secured by the thing bought, such as a house or car, at a low rate.
_Avoid_: Mortgage, secured credit

**Retail Credit**:
An unsecured Citizen Loan for everyday purchases such as groceries, at the high rate Commercial Banks profit from.
_Avoid_: Credit card, consumer loan

**Write-off**:
The loss a lender takes when a borrower defaults, which destroys money and is recorded as a money-destruction event.
_Avoid_: Bad debt, haircut

**Capacity**:
An Industry's production capital, bought as capital goods and lost to depreciation.
_Avoid_: Size, level

**Public Funding Share**:
The percentage of a service's cost, such as healthcare or education, that the Treasury pays to the Industry.
_Avoid_: Subsidy, voucher

**Sectoral Balance**:
The net money position of the Government, Households, Industries and Commercial Banks, which shows where Treasury surpluses drain demand.
_Avoid_: Money flow, balance sheet

**Product**:
A good or service an Industry sells. An Industry can make several kinds.
_Avoid_: Item, good

**Household Cohort**:
A group of citizens treated as one unit for simulation and display, with results equal to simulating each member individually.
_Avoid_: Family, population group, pop

**Citizen**:
One individual person, shown by zooming into a Household Cohort.
_Avoid_: Person, pop, agent

**Welfare Stats**:
The measures of a Household Cohort's condition: health, hunger, thirst, homelessness, education, and wealth.
_Avoid_: Happiness, needs

**Willingness to Pay**:
The most a Household Cohort would pay for a Product, set by need urgency, budget, taste and substitutes, and the basis of price discovery for every Product.
_Avoid_: Demand price, utility

**Experience Type**:
One of Trades, Professional or Scientist, a skill a Citizen builds by working, each boosting productivity in two specific Industries.
_Avoid_: Skill tier, job class, profession

**Owner Cohort**:
The Household Cohort that receives dividends from privately owned Industries.
_Avoid_: Shareholders, investors, capitalists

**Equity Stake**:
Government ownership in an Industry, converted from an Industry Loan, which pays dividends to the Treasury.
_Avoid_: Nationalisation, shares

**Tuition Loan**:
A Citizen Loan used to buy education, with a market-driven price and an optional cheap Government rate.
_Avoid_: Student loan

**Research**:
Progress on a Technology, at a rate set by the scientists employed in the Research Labs Industry.
_Avoid_: Tech progress, science

**Technology**:
A node in the tech tree that unlocks new Industries or Products once researched.
_Avoid_: Tech, upgrade

**Tick**:
One in-game day of simulation.
_Avoid_: Turn, step, frame

## Starting Industries

**Agriculture**:
Primary Industry that grows crops and raises animals to produce food Products.
_Avoid_: Farming, Agriculture and Food

**Mining and Energy**:
Primary Industry that extracts raw materials and generates power.
_Avoid_: Resources, utilities

**Manufacturing**:
Secondary Industry that turns raw materials into goods.
_Avoid_: Factories, industry (as a category)

**Construction**:
Secondary Industry that builds housing and infrastructure.
_Avoid_: Building

**Retail and Logistics**:
Tertiary Industry that distributes goods and sells them to Households.
_Avoid_: Shops, trade, transport

**Healthcare and Education**:
Public-service Industry that raises the health and education of Household Cohorts.
_Avoid_: Public services

**Research Labs**:
The Industry that employs scientists and drives Research.
_Avoid_: R&D, university
